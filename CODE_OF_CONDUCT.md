# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to report issues, ask questions, and contribute, regardless
of experience or background.

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work, assume good faith, and accept
constructive feedback. Help others participate without pressure or ridicule.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, doxxing, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests,
discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban
repeat or serious offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider
the context. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.

Report harmful public content using GitHub's **Report content** action. For
private concerns, use a contact method listed on the maintainer's
[profile](https://github.com/infinuts). Do not post private details in an issue.
Reports will be handled with care and shared only as needed to respond.
